FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is NHIMS?

NHIMS (National Health Information Management System) is Bangladesh's comprehensive digital healthcare platform designed to connect patients, doctors, hospitals, laboratories, and pharmacies for seamless medical care.

Is NHIMS secure?

Absolutely. NHIMS uses enterprise-grade encryption, secure cloud storage, and follows international healthcare data protection standards to keep your information safe.

Who can use NHIMS?

NHIMS is designed for everyone - patients, doctors, hospital administrators, laboratory staff, pharmacists, and government health authorities.

How can hospitals register?

Hospitals can register through the hospital admin portal. The registration process includes verification of hospital credentials and government approvals.

How can patients book appointments?

Patients can easily book appointments by creating an account, searching for their preferred doctor or hospital, selecting a time slot, and confirming the booking online.
