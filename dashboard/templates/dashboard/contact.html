FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions

How can hospitals register on NHIMS?

Hospitals can register through the hospital admin portal. The registration process includes submitting hospital credentials, licenses, and government approvals. Once verified, hospitals gain full access to the platform.

How can doctors get verified on NHIMS?

Doctors can register on the platform and submit their BMDC registration, qualifications, and experience details. The verification process is conducted by hospital administrators to ensure all credentials are authentic.

How can patients book appointments?

Patients can easily book appointments by creating an account, searching for their preferred doctor or hospital, selecting a time slot, and confirming the booking online. They will receive confirmation via SMS and email.

How long does hospital approval take?

Hospital approval typically takes 2-3 business days. The process involves verification of submitted documents and credentials. Once approved, hospitals receive confirmation and access to the platform.

How can I report a technical issue?

You can report technical issues by contacting our support team through email (support@nhims.gov.bd) or phone (+880 1700-000000). Our team will respond within 24 hours and resolve the issue as quickly as possible.
