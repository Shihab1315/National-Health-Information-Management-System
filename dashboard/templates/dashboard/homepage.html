FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is NHIMS?

NHIMS (National Health Information Management System) is Bangladesh's comprehensive digital healthcare platform designed to connect patients, doctors, hospitals, laboratories, and pharmacies for seamless medical care.

Is NHIMS free to use?

Yes, NHIMS is completely free for patients. Healthcare providers and hospitals may have subscription plans for premium features.

How do I book an appointment?

Simply create an account, search for your preferred doctor or hospital, select a time slot, and confirm your appointment. You'll receive a confirmation notification instantly.

Is my medical data secure?

Absolutely. NHIMS uses enterprise-grade encryption, secure cloud storage, and follows international healthcare data protection standards to keep your information safe.
